How basket sharing works

1 · A basket is just weights. A small .json file listing each stock and what percentage of the basket it should be — plus a reference price.

2 · Weights travel; share counts don't. You might invest ₹4,00,000 and a friend ₹80,000. The same file works for both, because the page turns weights into whole share counts at whatever amount you type.

3 · It tells you the truth about size. Every basket has a minimum below which the priciest stock can't get even one share. The page shows that minimum, and shows how far each holding lands from its target weight once rounded.

Good to know

No price feed. A page with no server can't look up live prices, and browsers block calling exchange APIs directly. So prices come from you — type them, or leave them out and the page will ask when you size the basket.

The dashboard needs your tradebook. Download it from your broker (for Zerodha: Console → Reports → Tradebook) and drag the file onto the page.

Sharing paid research. If a basket's constituents come from a paid subscription, that provider's terms usually restrict passing them on. Baskets you build yourself are yours.
