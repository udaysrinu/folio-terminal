Runs entirely in your browser

Someone shares a basket. You get your own buy list.

A basket is a list of stocks and target percentages — not share counts. Type what you want to invest and Folio Terminal turns those percentages into exact whole-share orders at your capital, not theirs.

Open the workbench I was sent a basket

No account. No server. Nothing uploaded. Not investment advice.

How it works

01 Load the sheet

Drag the .json a friend sent you onto the page, or paste the text. You immediately see how many stocks it holds and the minimum it needs.

02 Type your amount

Get share counts, total cost, and how far each holding lands from its target weight once rounded to whole shares.

03 Place the orders

Copy them into your broker by hand, or hand the whole basket to Zerodha Kite pre-filled — you review and confirm every order there.

The idea

You invest
₹4,00,000
StockSharesWeight
TCS3124.7%
RELIANCE9230.2%
HDFCBANK10345.1%
Your friend invests
₹80,000
StockSharesWeight
TCS624.1%
RELIANCE1829.7%
HDFCBANK2146.2%

Same file, same targets of 25 / 30 / 45% — and completely different share counts. That is the whole trick: percentages travel between people, share counts don't.

Notice the weights aren't identical either. Whole shares don't divide evenly, so the smaller amount drifts further from target — 1.2 points at ₹80,000 versus 0.3 at ₹4,00,000. The page shows you that drift rather than hiding it, and below this basket's minimum of ₹12,720 it stops you instead of quietly dropping TCS. (Illustrative prices.)

What it will not do

Fetch live prices

A page with no server can't call an exchange, and browsers block it anyway. Prices come from the sheet you were sent, or you type them from your broker app.

Shrink a basket silently

Below the minimum, the priciest stock can't get even one share. Rather than drop it and hand you a different portfolio, the page blocks and suggests amounts that work.

Give you advice

It does arithmetic. It doesn't know whether a basket is any good, and nothing is placed until you confirm it yourself in your own broker.
