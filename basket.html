Where do I get this file?
Zerodha, from Kite (quickest)
  1. Open kite.zerodha.com/holdings
  2. Above the table on the right there is a small download icon (a downward arrow). Click it and choose XLSX or CSV.
  3. Drop the downloaded file here.
Zerodha, from Console — console.zerodha.com/portfolio/holdings, then Download at the top right.
Any broker export works. It needs a symbol column and a quantity column; a price column is used when present. Or skip the file entirely and paste the rows below.

Easiest · paste your order-confirmation email

Fully offline — the text never leaves this page. This is the easiest route: the email already has the exact stocks, quantities and fill prices.

Or type the stocks yourself

Symbols alone give you an equal-weight basket. Prices are only needed to turn weights into share counts — you can add them now or when you size it.

Or turn what you already own into a basket

Makes a shareable sheet out of your current holdings: each stock's weight is its share of your portfolio's present value. Use this when you are the one sending a basket. (Comparing your holdings against a basket someone sent you is Step 5 on the other tab.)

Drop your holdings file XLSX / CSV
or click to choose one — nothing is uploaded, it is read in this tab
Where do I get this file?
Zerodha, from Kite (quickest)
  1. Open kite.zerodha.com/holdings
  2. Above the table on the right there is a small download icon (a downward arrow). Click it and choose XLSX or CSV.
  3. Drop the downloaded file here.
Zerodha, from Console (has cost prices too)
  1. Open console.zerodha.com/portfolio/holdings
  2. Click Download at the top right of the holdings table.
Any other broker works too. The file needs a symbol column, a quantity column, and a price column — headers can be named anything reasonable (Instrument, Qty., LTP, Avg. cost…) and extra columns are ignored.
No file? Select the holdings rows in your broker, copy them, and use the paste box in Step 5 on the other tab instead.
