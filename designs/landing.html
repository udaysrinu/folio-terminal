Someone shares a basket.
You get your own buy list.

Load a JSON file containing a list of target allocations. Type in how much you want to invest. Folio calculates exactly how many shares to buy to match those percentages, showing you the required trades and total cost before you open your broker.

Not investment advice. Output is for manual entry into your brokerage. No data leaves your machine.

HOW IT WORKS
upload_file

Load the sheet

Drag and drop the .json basket file you were sent. It contains a list of tickers and their target percentage weights.

calculate

Type your amount

Enter your total capital. Folio converts percentages into exact share counts and estimates the total cost based on yesterday's close.

receipt_long

Place the orders

Take the final generated list to your brokerage (like Zerodha Kite) and manually place the limit orders.

THE IDEA

Same logic, different scale.

Whether you are investing four lakhs or eighty thousand, the structural integrity of the basket remains identical. Only the absolute share counts change to fit your capital constraints.

WHAT IT WILL NOT DO
monitoring

No live prices

Estimations use end-of-day data. You must confirm current market prices before executing trades.

api

No automated execution

This tool does not connect to your broker's API. It produces a list for manual execution.

account_balance

No advice

We do not verify the quality of the baskets you are sent. We only provide the arithmetic to size them.
