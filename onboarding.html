Is it safe to put a key in a web page?

Yes — an api_key alone can't touch your account. It only identifies the app. Placing an order still needs your Zerodha login and 2FA, which happens on Kite's own site, and every order is shown to you for approval before anything executes. The dangerous half is the API secret — which this page never asks for and never uses.

What actually happens: the page builds a form and posts it to kite.zerodha.com/connect/basket. Your browser goes to Kite, Kite shows the basket, you confirm. The orders are placed by Kite, in your session — not by this page.
