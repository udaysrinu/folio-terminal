Basket tracker

Per-basket performance, grouped by the tag its orders were placed under

Drop any JSON that carries order tags — a Kite orders export, or a saved batch map. Orders are grouped by their tag, which is the only basket marker Kite records, and each group's name is yours to type. Prices come live; nothing is uploaded.

Kite sets a tag when an order is placed and never after, and holdings carry no tag at all — so snapshot your orders on the day you place them, or the grouping is gone.

Prices from this app's own /api/price. Unrealised figures only — realised returns and XIRR need a tradebook; dividends are not exposed by Kite's holdings or orders API.
